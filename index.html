CC2 Auto Clear

Adds the bed-clearing cycle to an ELEGOO Centauri Carbon 2 G-code, for the stock firmware or OpenCentauri COSMOS: the purge goes into the bin, and the print ends by cooling the bed, flexing the plate and sweeping the parts off the front. Everything runs in this page; your file never leaves the computer.
